Exploring Jagannath Puri, Odisha.



Welcome to Jaganaath Puri

Jagannath Puri is a beautiful holy city in Odisha, India. It is famous for the Jagannath Temple, beautiful beaches, culture and delicious food.


Konark Sun Temple

A UNESCO World Heritage Site known for its architecture.

Delicious Food of Puri

Puri is famous for its traditional food and Mahaprasad. During my visit, I enjoyed exploring the local food.

  • Mahaprasad
  • Khaja
  • Dalma
  • Rasagola

Puri Beech

A UNESCO World Heritage Site known for its architecture.



Jagannath Temple

A famous Hindu temple dedicated to Lord Jagannath.



My Travel Experience

My trip to Jagannath Puri was a wonderful experience. I enjoyed the beautiful beach, peaceful atmosphere, local culture and delicious food. It was a memorable journey for me.

Travel Tips

  • Carry comfortable clothes.
  • Keep drinking water with you.
  • Respect local culture and traditions.
  • Keep your surroundings clean.
