Delicious Masala Maggi

Maggi is one of the most popular and delicious snacks. It is easy to make and takes only 10 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 2 packets Maggi noodles
  • 2 Maggi Tastemaker sachets
  • 1 chopped onion
  • 1 chopped tomato
  • 1 green chilli
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 tabelspoon oil
  • Fresh Coriander leaves

How To Make Maggi

  1. Heat oil in a pan
  2. Add chopped onions and green chilli
  3. Add chopped tomatoes and vegetables
  4. Cook for 2 minutes
  5. Add 2 cups of water
  6. Add maggi noodles and tastmaker
  7. Cook for2-3 minutes
  8. Garnish with coriander leaves
  9. Serve hot and enjoy your maggi
